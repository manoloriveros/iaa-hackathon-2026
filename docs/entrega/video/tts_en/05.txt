To assess changes, I split the data by year and grouped linked customers and addresses. In the revised historical comparison, reporting completeness, category encodings, trees and adjustments were learned without evaluation data. We deliberately altered the held-out data and checked that the relevant models remained unchanged.

We compared models with and without additional factors, removed influential groups, and tested fifty scenarios for reserves and reporting delays. The overall difference stayed positive, with source models and reporting curves fixed. There were adverse findings: the observed cost of late-reported bodily injury claims exceeded projections. These time splits also informed selection, limiting my conclusions.
