A safeguard is a condition set before accepting a change. I required that overall gains should not conceal material deterioration for new business, considering the uncertainty interval.

Transfer learning improved performance overall, but did not establish that protection. After seeing the result, I relaxed the rule, accepting a non-negative point estimate of the improvement without requiring the interval to meet the original lower bound. Waiving that requirement allowed us to proceed by exception; the risk remained. Gains from locally estimated factors were also concentrated in renewals, without establishing stability for new business. I retained the submission on that exploratory basis.
